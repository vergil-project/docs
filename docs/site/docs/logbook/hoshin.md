# 方針 Hoshin

The vital few are the fleet's three to five objectives, deliberately not a
long list, each with a line on why it matters and how progress will be
judged. They are set at the yearly and quarterly `start` reviews by キャッチボール
catchball, the agent proposing and the human adjusting until both accept, and
every weekly review checks the work against them. This is a current document,
not a log: its history is its git history and the entries that changed it.

## Direction

Vergil is a personal engineering toolkit: the means of building tooling and
software that automates complex problems sustainably and securely, at an
enterprise organization's bar for standards, security, quality and
maintainability, scaled down to one person. It is a lab first, free to
experiment, and a factory that produces real things. The human's time goes
to designs, to breaking large undertakings into epics, and to saying "go";
the hoshin cadence is how one person steers it. The full direction is in
the [2026 yearly start](yearly/2026-start.md).
