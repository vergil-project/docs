    Decisions¶

    The vital few were set for the first time, by catchball, from the yearly start's objectives for the rest of 2026; they are now in hoshin.md:

    1. Run the cadence.
    2. Drain the queues (vergil-project's, for Q4).
    3. Keep the fleet green.
    4. Plan 2027.

    The capacity plan:

    1. Capacity: about 10 epics a week, so roughly 60 in the six weeks to 2027-01-04 if the pace holds; the pace is rising.
    2. Finish first: vergil-project/.github#1384, the accelerator, then vergil-project/.github#1318 and vergil-project/.github#1440. vergil-project/.github#1374, vergil-project/.github#375 and vergil-project/.github#388 advance as they fit.
    3. The planning burn-down: two or three small or medium items a day, 10 to 15 a week, enough to reach 20 or fewer net of new inflow. The risk is inflow: 42 intake items arrived in four days. Large and extra-large items wait unless they make planning itself faster.
    4. December: brainstorm the forge migration and Vergil 3 through epic-create.
    5. Security records: one cleanup pass first, closing the stale records; vergil-project/.github#724 (hardening the security-records model) waits until the cleanup shows what is live.
    6. Does not fit this quarter: the logical-minds-foundry backlog. Its intake gets one epic-review pass, deferring the MQ items with the blocker that MQ work resumes once Vergil 2 is rounded out.

    Kaizen¶

    • The delivery-rate check took 168 search calls, run from a scratch script. It is the clearest candidate for a vrg-* tool (history, vergil-project/.github#1317), and that tool should also draw the charts that show how the rate of progress is changing over time.
    • vrg-queue --json gives no ages or size summary; a scratch script computed them. The Queue size and age check needs that summary from the tool.
    • The queue checks should report a missing queue epic as a finding rather than an error, as mnemosys-project's planning and deferred queues showed.
