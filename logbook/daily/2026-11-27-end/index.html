    終礼 shūrei: daily end 2026-11-27¶

    Friday from its chōrei to the late afternoon. Most of the release-lock epic's tasks landed, the model-tiering epic landed its first agents, and five releases were cut. One scheduled configuration sync failed on a tool download.

    Findings¶

    Merged PRs. 53 merged in the window: 51 in vergil-project and 2 in mnemosys-project. In vergil-project, the user agent submitted 38, in vergil-tooling (22), vergil-claude-plugin (9), .github (4), vergil-actions (1), docs (1) and vergil-fixture-python-lib (1). The human submitted 11 release, back-merge and dependency PRs. Automation submitted 2, a managed-files sync and an upstream refresh in vergil-containers. The 2 in mnemosys-project were managed-files syncs. The day's themes were the release lock (vergil-project/.github#1664), with about fifteen of its tasks merged; the first model-tiering agents and routing (vergil-project/.github#1823); and the published specs and plans for model tiering and the batch release unit.

    Closed issues and epics. No epic closed. On the day, 62 issues closed in vergil-project, 1 in logical-minds-foundry and none in mnemosys-project.

    Releases. vergil-tooling v2.1.351 to v2.1.353; vergil-claude-plugin v2.1.98 and v2.1.99. vergil-tooling v2.1.354 was published four minutes after the window closed.

    Failed and cancelled runs.

    • Reported. vergil-project/vergil-containers#1042 (nightly-rebuild-dev) reported a recovery at 12:15. vergil-project/vergil-containers#1063 (image-publish) was opened for a CD run cancelled by a newer push, and reported a recovery at 12:29.
    • Open queue. 2 open ops issues, both in vergil-project and both recovered and awaiting a close: vergil-project/vergil-containers#1042 and vergil-project/vergil-containers#1063. logical-minds-foundry, mnemosys-project and mq-rest-admin-project have no ops issues; diogenes-project and vergils-nemesis still have no operations epic.
    • Not reported. mnemosys-project/.github's scheduled Ops run failed in its github-config job. The cause was not a configuration finding: the job's own sync PR, mnemosys-project/.github#136, failed its CI when a tool download was refused (HTTP 403), so the job's wait for the merge failed. Three vergil-tooling CI runs on feature branches failed their version check just after a release was cut; both branches merged later.

    Security and audit signals. No security finding in the window. The one red github-config job is the sync failure above. Every other Ops run in the window passed.

    Open security records. 63 open across the fleet, up from 54 this morning. This review's findings touch none.

    Handed-off work.

    • No branch is reported ready and unsubmitted, and no relay refs remain.
    • Open: vergil-project/vergil-tooling#5368, the v2.1.354 back-merge; mnemosys-project/.github#136; and, unchanged, logical-minds-foundry/mq-resiliency-lab-for-linux#1682.
    • Four worktrees have no PR: in packages, vergil-actions, vergil-python and .github, from 4 to 52 days old, one on a branch that is gone.

    Rulings awaiting the human. The merge-review ruling request posted on vergil-project/vergil-claude-plugin#1141 on Wednesday evening is still unanswered. Today's lists on vergil-project/.github#1664 and vergil-project/.github#99 were ruled.

    Decisions¶

    • The github-config failure is recorded as a sync failure, not a security-related one: its log shows no configuration finding.
    • The human reran the failed CI of mnemosys-project/.github#136.
    • No follow-ups are filed tonight.

    Note for tomorrow's chōrei.

    • Answer the merge-review ruling request on vergil-project/vergil-claude-plugin#1141.
    • Close the two recovered ops issues, vergil-project/vergil-containers#1042 and vergil-project/vergil-containers#1063.
    • Explain the rise in open security records from 54 to 63 (vergil-project/.github#1842).
    • Prune the four stale worktrees with no PR.

    Follow-ups¶

    None.
